# Android source license map

Existing file licenses and notices are retained. MAP/OpenCPN keeps its GPLv2-or-later and mixed per-file licenses,with original COPYING/LICENSING/data/copyright and third-party notices. It is not relabeled wholesale as GPLv3. The own shared-eq kernel remains MPL-2.0 as its Cargo.toml declares. Gradle wrapper is official Apache-2.0 tool code. Barlow fonts retain SIL OFL1.1; DejaVu retains its own bundled license. David Li’s adapted FFT portions retain their MIT notice in helm/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

For own source without an earlier overriding license,the requested publication grant is GPL-3.0-only. Own prose and own artwork are shared under CC-BY-SA-4.0 following Christoph’s explicit 1 November 2026 instruction; the older helm/licenses/ASSET_RIGHTS.md remains as provenance,not a replacement for the new grant or for upstream rights. This grants no rights to private charts,OEM binaries,Spotify content,third-party brands or account data.

See per-file manifest.json and the bundled upstream license texts. This is source-only; APK dependency notices must be collected for any subsequent binary distribution.
