# Foundations and third-party notices

Number Five builds on existing work. Specific source notices inside snapshots prevail over this overview.

- BBN Marine OS / bareboat-necessities: original marine-system base.
- AvNav: original navigation/integration path; not a claim to have written AvNav.
- Signal K / resources provider: shared boat data and route resources.
- OpenCPN / S52 / GDAL: chart rendering foundations and the original ported code. Preserve GPL and dependency notices in the Android package.
- NOAA: Boston ENC data used in the labelled experimental chart studies. These historical test images do not replace current official navigational material.
- librespot / Raspotify / ALSA: Spotify Connect and Linux audio foundations. The existing receiver has MIT notices; crate licences apply individually.
- CamillaDSP / Henrik Enquist: digital audio processing. No complete Camilla distribution or account is included merely by linking the project.
- Raspberry Pi Pico SDK: Pico foundation; host-source availability does not imply the latest transmit firmware is bundled.
- Leaflet1.9.4: BSD-2-Clause; vendor notices retained in GPS live-map source.
- Android, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and existing font/art notices remain applicable to app sources and images.
- build123d / OCCT, KiCad, ngspice and historical Fusion work: CAD/electronics tools; own sources do not relicense those tools.
- The included Sites/Vinext starter retains its vendored Vite-plugin licence and dependency lockfile. React, Vinext, Cloudflare tooling and other dependencies keep their upstream package licences.

Image identifiers, origins, kinds and SHA-256 values are in `docs/media-provenance.json`. Images are copied unchanged. Simulators, host-renderer studies, historical CAD, physical hardware photos and an actual Lumina sonar screenshot are distinguished in captions.
