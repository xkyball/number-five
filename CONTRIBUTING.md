# Gemeinsam weiterbauen / Contributing

Contributions include practical builds, printing, wiring, suppliers, translations, source improvements and reproducible test reports. Fabrication, installation and commercial services are welcome under the applicable licences.

1. Identify the module and exact source revision. For hardware, include the actual device/part variant and the conditions of your measurement.
2. Explain the problem and intended result. Keep changes focused and preserve existing interfaces or clearly describe a proposed change.
3. Record what you checked and what remains untested. A successful build does not establish device, electrical, acoustic or on-water behaviour.
4. Preserve upstream attribution and licences. Identify new original contributions and their applicable licence. Do not upload credentials, real account data, licensed charts or proprietary OEM software.
5. AI-assisted contributions are welcome. State meaningful AI use and review the resulting code, diagrams and assumptions yourself.

No contributor agreement or transfer of ownership is required by this project. Submit source changes as a pull request; use the website contact form for private questions or practical offers. Existing snapshots retain their own development/test constraints.
